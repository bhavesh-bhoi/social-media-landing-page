FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Can't see your question? Send us a message below and we will answer it personally.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. If you use another platform, ask us and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

Do I approve content before it goes live?

Yes. Unless you tell us otherwise, we share posts, reels and captions with you for approval before publishing.

Who pays for the ads?

Ad budgets are separate from our fees. You decide the budget, it is paid to the platform, and we never spend beyond what you approve.

Can you guarantee followers or sales?

No honest agency can. Platforms change their rules often. What we promise is consistent, on-brand work and clear reports on what is working.

How do you access my accounts?

Through each platform's official roles, such as admin or partner access. We never need your passwords.
