Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aina Tech Services LLP ("Aina", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit our website, contact us or use our social media Services. We respect your privacy and handle your information in line with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you fill in our contact or appointment form, email us or call us, we collect details such as your name, company name, address, how you heard about us, email address, phone number and preferred meeting date, along with anything you write to us.
  • Information from your social accounts: if you become a client, we may access your social media accounts and their insights (for example reach, engagement and audience data) through the platforms' official access tools, only to deliver the Services.
  • Technical information: basic data your browser sends to any website, such as IP address, browser type and the pages you open.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries, schedule appointments and give you quotations.
  • To plan, create, publish and report on social media content for you.
  • To keep our website secure and working properly.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Contact and appointment form

The form on our website is hosted on our own customer management system (ainacrm.ainatech.in). The details you enter are stored there and are used only for the purposes above.

4. Cookies and third-party resources

Our website does not use advertising cookies. To display the page, your browser loads fonts and scripts from third-party providers (such as Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr), and those providers may receive your IP address and browser details. If we add analytics or similar tools in future, we will update this policy.

5. Sharing your information

We share personal information only when needed to provide our Services (for example with hosting, email, design or advertising platforms that work with us), when you ask us to, or when the law requires it. We ask the providers we use to protect your information.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, including to follow up on enquiries, deliver projects and meet legal or accounting requirements. After that we delete it or anonymise it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. We ask clients to grant access to social accounts through official role-based access instead of sharing passwords. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • erase it, where we are not required to keep it; and
  • withdraw your consent to our using it.

To make a request or raise a concern, write to us at info@ainatech.in. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

Our Services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Links to other websites

Our website may link to other websites and social platforms. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Contact us at info@ainatech.in or on +91 82007 70942.
